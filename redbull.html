Team Origins

Oracle Red Bull Racing entered Formula 1 in 2005 after Red Bull acquired the Jaguar Racing team. What began as a bold experiment quickly became one of the most successful teams in modern F1. Known for innovation, fearless strategy, and elite drivers, Red Bull Racing redefined what a young team could achieve.

Key Milestones

2005

Red Bull Racing debuts in Formula 1.

2010–2013

Four consecutive Constructors’ and Drivers’ Championships with Sebastian Vettel.

2021

Max Verstappen wins a dramatic World Championship.

2022–2023

Red Bull dominates the hybrid era with record-breaking seasons.
