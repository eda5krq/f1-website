Team Origins

Aston Martin Aramco Formula One Team combines iconic racing heritage with modern engineering. Built around aerodynamic efficiency, high-speed stability, and continuous development, the team competes at the top level of motorsport with relentless ambition.

Key Milestones

1922

Aston Martin founded and begins building a racing legacy.

1959

Le Mans victory cements Aston Martin’s endurance heritage.

2021

Aston Martin returns to F1 under the modern team identity.

2023+

Major performance rise and continued investment in facilities and development.
