McLAREN RACING

The Pioneers of Formula 1

Team Origins

McLaren Racing was founded in 1963 by New Zealand racing driver Bruce McLaren. Starting as a small team, McLaren quickly became a powerhouse in Formula 1, known for innovation, engineering excellence, and legendary drivers. With 8 Constructors' Championships and 12 Drivers' Championships, McLaren is one of the most successful teams in F1 history.

Key Milestones

1966

McLaren's first Formula 1 victory at the Monaco Grand Prix.

1974

First Constructors' Championship with Emerson Fittipaldi.

1976

James Hunt wins the Drivers' Championship.

1980s

Dominance with Alain Prost and Ayrton Senna, winning multiple titles.
