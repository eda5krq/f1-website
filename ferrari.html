Team Origins

Scuderia Ferrari is Formula 1’s most iconic team—defined by heritage, innovation, and relentless racing spirit. Since its first Grand Prix entry, Ferrari has combined Italian design and engineering with world-class competition, becoming a symbol of passion and performance across generations.

Key Milestones

1950

Ferrari competes in the first ever Formula 1 World Championship season.

1975–1979

Championship success returns with legendary cars and drivers.

2000–2004

Dominant era with multiple Drivers’ and Constructors’ Championships.

2022+

A modern resurgence focused on innovation, speed, and returning to the top.
