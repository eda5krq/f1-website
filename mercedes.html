Team Origins

Mercedes has a legendary Formula 1 history, returning as a works team in the modern era and becoming a benchmark for engineering excellence. Known for innovation, relentless performance, and championship-winning drivers, Mercedes-AMG PETRONAS Formula One Team defined a generation of Formula 1 success.

Key Milestones

1954–1955

Mercedes returns and wins with Juan Manuel Fangio in a historic era.

2010

Mercedes returns as a factory team in the modern era.

2014–2020

An era of dominance with multiple Constructors’ and Drivers’ Championships.

2021+

Continued innovation, evolution, and pursuit of championship performance.
