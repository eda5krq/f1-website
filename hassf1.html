HAAS F1 TEAM

American Power on the F1 Grid

Team Origins

Haas F1 Team is the first American-led Formula 1 team in the modern era. Founded by Gene Haas, the team entered Formula 1 with a bold vision: efficiency, engineering focus, and competitive spirit on the world stage.

Key Milestones

2016

Haas F1 Team debuts in Formula 1 with strong points finishes.

2018

Best Constructors’ Championship result: 5th place.

2022

Major comeback with new regulations and fresh lineup.

2024+

Focused rebuild aiming for midfield competitiveness.
